Privacy & Data

SyntraAid is built with privacy as a priority, for volunteers and donors alike. This page explains, in plain terms, how the platform handles data.

What we collect

Account details (name, email, role), volunteer profile information (skills, availability, bio), project and task records, and attendance logs. We do not process payment or financial transaction data.

How volunteer data is protected

Donor-facing views never include volunteer personal contact details such as email addresses or phone numbers. Donors see participation as counts and hours only.

The activity log

Significant actions are recorded in a tamper-proof activity log with timestamps. These entries cannot be edited or deleted by any user, which keeps the record trustworthy.

Attendance records

Attendance is insert-only. Records are never edited or deleted; corrections are made by adding a new entry that references the original. Total hours are always computed from these records.

Your data

Your account information is held securely and is not shared with other users beyond what each role is permitted to see. To update your details or ask about your data, contact your organisation's administrator.
