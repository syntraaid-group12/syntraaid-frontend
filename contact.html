Who is SyntraAid for?

Tell us which role fits you in the form above. Here is what each one does on the platform.

NGO Admin

Runs the organisation account. Invites users, oversees all projects, manages donor access and generates impact reports.

Coordinator

Leads projects on the ground. Creates and assigns tasks, manages volunteers and verifies attendance.

Volunteer

Does the work. Views assigned tasks, updates progress and logs attendance hours from web or mobile.

Donor

Funds and follows projects. Sees progress, program health and contribution summaries, never volunteers' personal contact details.
