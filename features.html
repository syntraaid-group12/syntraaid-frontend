Features

Everything SyntraAid does, grounded in the work NGOs actually need to coordinate and report.

Volunteer Management

Profiles with skills and availability. Search and assign volunteers to projects by skill or day.

Projects & Milestones

Create projects with goals, timelines and milestones. A project cannot go active until a coordinator is assigned.

Task Tracking

Move tasks through Not Started, In Progress, Blocked and Completed. Blocked tasks alert the coordinator immediately.

Attendance & Hours

Insert-only attendance logging. Total hours are always computed from records, never edited by hand.

Transparency Dashboard

A tamper-proof activity log of significant actions with timestamps, that no user can edit or delete.

Donor Visibility

Donors follow funded projects with progress and program health, never volunteers' personal details.

Impact Reports

Admins generate impact, attendance and KPI reports and export them as PDF or CSV.

In-App Notifications

Task assignments, deadline reminders, blocked-task alerts to coordinators and milestone alerts to donors.
