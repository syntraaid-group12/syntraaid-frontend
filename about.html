About SyntraAid

A transparency-focused NGO and volunteer management platform, bringing coordination, accountability and impact reporting into one place.

SyntraAid is a transparency-focused NGO and volunteer management platform. It brings coordination, accountability and impact reporting into one place, so organisations can run their programmes effectively and show funders exactly what their support achieves.

The problem

Many NGOs coordinate volunteers and track project work across scattered spreadsheets and chats. Hours go unrecorded, progress is hard to prove, and donors lack clear visibility into impact.

Our approach

One coordinated system: manage volunteers and projects, log attendance with hours computed automatically, and give donors a read-only window into real, tamper-proof progress.

What we value

Transparency

A tamper-proof activity log records significant actions, so the record stays trustworthy.

Accountability

Volunteer hours are always computed from real attendance records, never edited by hand.

Privacy

Donors see progress and impact, never volunteers' personal contact details.
